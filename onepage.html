Crossing Watch

The bridge has gone dark. A lone ranger carries a crystal staff from the south bank to a watchfire beyond the pass. The creatures following the fog are already there.

On the trail

Speak to Mira, clear the hounds, and hold three waybeacons. Across the bridge, interrupt the Warden’s Mistcall. In Ashwood, silence three shrines and defeat the Sentinel. Back at the ferry, take nine field orders. Follow wagon tracks, bring the brass bell home, read the prisoner roll and heal missing scouts. The trail ends at the Barrow King.

One staff, two decisions

Each staff hit earns Spirit. Spend it on a Seal to interrupt an enemy heal, or keep it to heal yourself. A violet thread gives the caster away. Dodge too early and a hound can catch you with its second bite.

Progression and audience

Nine choices of three talents let you build around melee, interrupts, or survival. The prototype is for players who enjoy reading MMO fights and want a short solo adventure. Across two opening chapters and 45 field assignments, a melee build finished in 33 minutes of active play in an automated run. A defensive build took 58. Checkpoints record each encounter.

Commercial direction

A proposed paid solo Steam release would expand the watch route with authored encounters. Month one covers movement and encounter tools. Months two and three add routes and bosses. Month four covers player tests and balancing. Month five is reserved for performance, fixes, and release.

Built with TypeScript, Three.js, and Vite. The designer chose the third-person format, combat references, talent rewards, and balance changes through playtesting. Codex helped implement the game and run regression checks. Character models and animations are by Quaternius.
